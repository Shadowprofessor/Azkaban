How scoring works

Each finding starts at 20 for Low, 45 for Medium, 70 for High, or 95 for Critical risk. A bounded record-count adjustment adds up to 14 points, with a maximum score of 100. The overall score is the highest finding score. A score describes exposure in the supplied records. Confidence is reported separately.
